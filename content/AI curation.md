
We use AI as a research tool and publish the results.

## Our Process

The essays on this website are generated through extensive conversations with Claude Opus 4.5, Anthropic's large language model. We recognize that AI-assisted writing is contentious, and we share some of that wariness—which is why we've chosen radical transparency over quiet use.

Each essay here emerges from extended, iterative exchange. This involves formulating questions, challenging initial responses, requesting elaboration or correction, synthesizing across multiple conversations, and exercising critical judgment throughout. An essay may represent hours of directed exploration. It is closer to conducting a research interview with an extraordinarily well-read interlocutor than to receiving an automated output.

We hold these essays to the same standards we would hold conventional writing. Our process involves:

- Iterative refinement across multiple exchanges
- Critical evaluation of claims and arguments
- Rejection of outputs that fail to meet our standards
- Selection based on genuine intellectual interest

## Language Models as Collective Intelligence

A large language model compresses patterns of human reasoning deposited across vast quantities of text and makes them queryable. When we engage with such a model, we are not consulting a single author but tapping into a distillation of collective human thought.

Wikipedia's value emerges from distributed contributions, editorial oversight, and accumulated revision—no single author, yet genuine intellectual substance. Language models represent a new kind of interface to a even broader collective inheritance. The person who queries, evaluates, and curates is participating in the process. By publishing high-quality syntheses with transparent attribution, we hope to modestly contribute to it.

## Why Curation Matters

We do not claim conventional originality; the ideas here are not ours in the way a novel argument would be. But directing inquiry, exercising judgment, and curating results constitute genuine intellectual contributions—ones with honorable precedent. 

The contribution of a literature review lies in selection, organization, and the identification of patterns across sources. Editors shape books profoundly without writing them. Intellectual life benefits from exploring ideas, synthesizing them and making them accessible.

If you find value in what you read here, we will consider this experiment a success.
